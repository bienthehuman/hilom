Patient header

Tops every patient and encounter screen: name (last name first), MRN, age and sex, and the ward and bed while admitted. Active severe allergies show in a banner that can't be dismissed. Under two years, age is in months; under a month, in days.

Vitals

ui-vital-value shows a reading with its unit. Out of the normal range it turns warning with an arrow; in the critical range, destructive and bold. The direction is also read out, so color never carries it alone. ui-trend-chart plots readings over time on one axis, with the normal range shaded and out-of-range points ringed. Hover it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Two series use chart-1 and chart-4, the pair checked for color-blind readers in both themes. Always show the readings in a table too.

Beds

ui-bed-picker chooses one bed, grouped by ward. Pass only the beds that can be chosen; admitting passes the free ones. They're native radio buttons, so Tab enters a ward and the arrow keys move between its beds. Each is read out with its ward. The chosen bed uses primary.

Voiding

Clinical entries are never deleted. uiConfirmAction goes on the button: it opens an alert dialog and, with requireReason, asks why before running the action. It stays open with the error if the action fails. A voided entry is struck through in muted-foreground with ui-voided-pill, which shows the reason on hover and focus. Vitals, notes and the MAR all use the same flow.
